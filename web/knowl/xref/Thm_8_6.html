Theorem 8.5.3.

Let \(P\) and \(Q\) be polynomials of degree \(m\) and \(n\text{,}\) respectively, where \(n \ge m+1\text{,}\) and let \(Q\) have simple zeros at the points \(t_1, \, t_2, \, \ldots, \, t_l \,\) on the \(x\)-axis. If \(\alpha\) is a positive real number and if \(f(z) =\frac{\exp(i\alpha z)P(z)}{Q(z)}\text{,}\) then
\begin{align} \text{ P.V. } \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\cos \alpha x\,dx \amp = -2\pi \sum\limits_{j=1}^k\mathrm{Im}(\mathrm{Res}[f,z_j])\notag\\ \amp - \pi \sum\limits_{j=1}^{l}\mathrm{Im}(\mathrm{Res}[f,t_j]),\tag{8.5.2} \end{align}
and
\begin{align} \text{ P.V. } \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\sin \alpha x\,dx \amp = 2\pi \sum\limits_{j=1}^k\mathrm{\mathrm{Re}}(\mathrm{Res}[f,z_j])\notag\\ \amp + \pi \sum_{j=1}^{l}\mathrm{\mathrm{Re}}(\mathrm{Res}[f,t_j]),\text{,}\tag{8.5.3} \end{align}
where \(z_1, \, z_2, \, \ldots ,z_k\) are the poles of \(f\) that lie in the upper half-plane.
