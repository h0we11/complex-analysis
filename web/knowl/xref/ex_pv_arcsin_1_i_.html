Example 9.4.6.

Find the principal value \(\mathrm{Arcsin}(1+i)\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Using Formulas (9.4.4) and (9.4.5), we get
\begin{align*} u(1,1) \amp =\mathrm{Arcsin}\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\right) \approx 0.666239432, \text{ and }\\ v(1,1) \amp =\mathrm{Arccosh}\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{2}\right) \approx 1.061275062\text{.} \end{align*}
🔗
Therefore, we have
\begin{equation*} \mathrm{Arcsin}(1+i) \approx0.666239432+i1.061275062\text{.} \end{equation*}
