Theorem 11.7.4. Shifting the Variable \(\boldsymbol t\).

If \(F(s)\) is the Laplace transform of \(f(t)\) and \(a \ge 0\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \mathcal{L}\big(U_a(t)f(t-a)\big) = e^{-as}F(s)\text{,} \end{equation*}
where \(f(t)\) and \(U_a(t)(t-a)\) are illustrated in Figure 11.7.5.
