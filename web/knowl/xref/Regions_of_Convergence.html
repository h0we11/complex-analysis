Theorem 4.4.1.

Suppose that \(f(z)=\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(z-\alpha )^n\text{.}\) Then the set of points \(z\) for which the series converges is one of the following:
  1. the single point \(z=\alpha\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. the disk \(D_{\rho}(\alpha ) =\{z:|z-\alpha |\lt \rho \}\text{,}\) along with part (either none, some, or all) of the circle \(C_{\rho}(\alpha ) =\{z:|z-\alpha|=\rho\}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. the entire complex plane.
