Example 5.1.3.

For any integer \(n\text{,}\) the points
\begin{equation*} z_n=\frac{5}{4}+i\left(\frac{11\pi}{6}+2n\pi\right) \end{equation*}
in the \(z\) plane are mapped onto the single point
\begin{align*} w_0 \amp = \exp(z_n)\\ \amp = e^{\frac{5}{4}}\left(\cos \frac{11\pi}{6}+i\sin \frac{11\pi}{6}\right)\\ \amp = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}e^{\frac{5}{4}}-i\frac{1}{2}e^{\frac{5}{4}}\\ \amp \approx 3.02-1.75i \end{align*}
in the \(w\) plane, as indicated in Figure 5.1.4.
