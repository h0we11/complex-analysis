Exercise 4.

Let \(U(t) =e^{-\frac{t^2}{2}}\text{.}\) Show that \(\mathfrak{F}\big(U(t)\big) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{w^2}{2}}\text{.}\)
Hint.
Use the integral definition and combine the terms in the exponent. Then complete the square and use the fact that \(\int\nolimits_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-\frac{r^2}{2}}\,dt=\sqrt{2\pi}\text{.}\)
