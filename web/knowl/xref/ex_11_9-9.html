Task 10.9.3.g.
Show that \(w=f(z)=(z-1)^{\alpha}\left[\frac{1+\alpha z}{1-\alpha}\right]^{1-\alpha}\) maps the upper half-plane \(\mathrm{Im}(z) >0\) onto the upper half-plane \(\mathrm{Im}(w)>0\) slit along the segment from \(0\) to \(e^{i\alpha \pi}\text{,}\) per Figure 10.9.18.
🔗
Hint.
Show that \(\, f\,'(z) = A\left[\frac{z+(1-\alpha)}{\alpha} \right]^{-\alpha}(z)(z-1)^{\alpha -1}.\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Select \(x_1 = -\frac{1-\alpha}{\alpha }\) and \(x_2=x_3=1\text{,}\) then form \(f\,'(z) = A(z+\frac{1-\alpha}{\alpha })^{-\alpha}(z)(z-1)^{\alpha -1}\text{.}\) Computation then reveals that \(A=(\frac{1-\alpha }{\alpha})^{\alpha -1}\text{,}\) which is used to construct the desired function:
\begin{align*} w \amp = f(z)\\ \amp =\int A(z+\frac{1-\alpha}{\alpha })^{-\alpha}(z)(z-1)^{\alpha -1}\,dz\\ \amp = (z-1)^{\alpha}(1+ \frac{\alpha z}{1-\alpha})^{1-\alpha}. \end{align*}
