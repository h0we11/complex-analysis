Corollary 4.3.2.

If \(|z|>1\) the series \(\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}z^{-n}\) converges to \(f(z) =\frac{1}{z-1}\) That is, if \(|z|>1\) then
\begin{align*} \sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}z^{-n} \amp = z^{-1}+z^{-2}+\cdots +z^{-n}+\cdots = \frac{1}{z-1}, \quad \text{or equivalently},\\ -\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}z^{-n} \amp = -z^{-1}-z^{-2}-\cdots -z^{-n}-\cdots = \frac{1}{1-z}\text{.} \end{align*}
🔗
If \(|z|\le 1\text{,}\) the series diverges.
