Theorem 11.6.1. Differentiation of \(\boldsymbol{f(t)}\).

Let \(f(t)\) and \(f\,'(t)\) be continuous for \(t \ge 0\text{,}\) and of exponential order. Then,
\begin{equation*} \mathcal{L}(\,f\,'(t)) = sF(s) -f(0), \quad \text{where} \quad F(s) = \mathcal{L}\big(\,f(t)\big)\text{.} \end{equation*}
