Theorem 6.5.1. Cauchy integral formula for \(\boldsymbol{f(z_0)}\).

Let \(f\) be analytic in the simply connected domain \(D\) and let \(C\) be a simple closed positively oriented contour in \(D\text{.}\) If \(z_0\) lies interior to \(C\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation} f(z_0)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_C\frac{f(z)}{z-z_0}\,dz\text{.}\tag{6.5.1} \end{equation}
