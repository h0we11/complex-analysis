Theorem 4.1.16.

Let \(z_n=x_n+iy_n\) and \(S=U+iV\text{.}\) Then
\begin{align*} S \amp = \sum\limits_{n=1}^\infty z_n=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}(x_n+iy_n) \quad \text{iff}\\ U \amp = \sum\limits_{n=1}^\infty x_n \quad \text{and} \quad V=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}y_n\text{.} \end{align*}
