Definition 7.4.2. Zero of order \(\boldsymbol{k}\).

A function \(f\) analytic in \(D_{R}(\alpha)\) has a zero of order \(\boldsymbol{k}\) at the point \(\alpha\) iff
\begin{equation*} f^{(n)}(\alpha)=0, \quad \text{for} \quad n=0,\,1,\ldots , k-1, \quad \text{but} \quad f^{(k)}(\alpha) \ne 0\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
A zero of order 1 is called a simple zero.
